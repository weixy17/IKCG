Codes refer to C. Liu, J. Yuen, and A. Torralba, “Sift flow: Dense correspondence across scenes and its applications,” IEEE Trans. Pattern Anal. Mach. Intell., vol. 33, no. 5, pp. 978–994, Aug. 2011.


You can choose to 1. use main_siftflowmap.m to generate single-scale dense SIFT features; 2. use main_multi_siftflowmap.m and then gl_multi_dimention_bilinear_interpolation.m to generate multiscale dense SIFT features.
